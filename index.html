WHY MATHORA

A score tells you what happened.
A diagnostic explains why.

Students often lose marks for very different reasons. Mathora captures both the selected answer and the reasoning behind it.

01

See the misconception

A wrong option can identify the error. The student’s working confirms whether it came from arithmetic, notation, method or understanding.

02

Match the right course

Different tests load for each qualification, exam board and level. Foundation and Core students do not receive Higher or HL content.

03

Measure exam readiness

The timer records the total duration and time spent on every question, revealing hesitation as well as accuracy.
