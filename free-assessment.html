BUILT FOR BETTER DECISIONS

Useful before tutoring, revision, mocks or an important exam.

A student can arrive at a correct answer through a fragile method, or lose marks despite understanding the underlying mathematics. Looking at both answer and working helps separate these situations.

  • Identify topic gaps and underlying misconceptions
  • Distinguish knowledge gaps from exam-technique issues
  • Review whether working is mathematically secure and efficient
  • Spot time-management problems across the diagnostic
  • Provide a clearer starting point for targeted support
