PRIVACY

How Mathora uses submitted information.

This page is a starter privacy notice and should be reviewed before public launch.

Information collected

Mathora may collect contact details, course information, assessment answers, submitted working and enquiry messages.

Purpose

Information is used to deliver diagnostics, review work, respond to enquiries and provide requested academic services.

Storage

Website submissions are stored using Supabase. Access should be restricted to authorised Mathora administrators.

Before launch

Add the registered business details, retention period, lawful basis, data-subject rights and contact information appropriate to the countries in which Mathora operates.
